Application forms

On Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and Workable application pages jobhunter draws small buttons beside the resume and cover letter fields. Show buttons controls them. Record submits controls watching for your own submit so jobhunter can ask whether you applied. They are independent, and each ATS has its own.

A posting is read only when you click the toolbar icon, press Alt+Shift+J or use the right-click Send to jobhunter, and sent only to your console on 127.0.0.1. Nothing is scored until you confirm. On the application pages above the extension runs on load, but writes nothing into the form and sends nothing about the page.
